How to play

Moving

  • Phone or tablet: ◀ ▶ run, ▲ jump (hold it to jump higher), hold LIGHT for your flashlight, POTION to drink one.
  • Keyboard: A D or arrows to run, W or Space to jump, F flashlight, E potion, Esc pause, M sound.

Hearts and potions

  • You have 3 hearts. Monsters, lava, spikes and falling each cost one. Lose them all and you go back to the last TV you touched.
  • Pick up empty beakers, then touch a bubbling cauldron to fill them. Each potion gives back a heart.
  • Hardcore: turn it on in the channel list. One hit and the course starts over.

The course

  • Touching a TV saves your spot. Springs bounce you high. Cracked blocks fall a moment after you step on them.
  • Run into a minecart to get in. It rolls the way you're facing. Tap ▲ to jump the cart. When it stops, tap ▲ to hop out.
  • Water: tap ▲ to swim up. Currents push you (and carts and monsters) along. Bubbles push you up.

Monsters

  • Flashlight: hold the light on a monster until it pops. Big ones take longer.
  • Watcher: red screen means its eye is opening. Hide behind a wall, or wear the sunglasses.
  • Lurker: only moves when you look away. Rolling Giant: rolls back and forth. Stay above it.
  • Gummy Worms: wiggle toward you. Wizard: sparkles, then shoots lightning. Walls block it.
  • Baba Yaga: swoops down from the sky. Alien: its beam lifts you up. Duck under a roof. Bigfoot: charges when he sees you, but he can't jump lava.

Playing with friends

  • Host: tap Invite friends (in the channel list or the pause menu) and Get an invite code. Then pick any course, and your friends play it with you.
  • Friend: on the main menu, type your name and the code under Play with friends, and tap Join.
  • Everyone sees each other run. When anyone pops a monster with their flashlight, it pops for everyone.

Building

  • Pick a piece at the bottom, then tap or drag to place it. Move slides around, Eraser removes things, Scene changes the background.
  • ▶ Test plays your course. While testing, tap FLY (or G) to fly around: you can't get hurt. Courses save themselves. Share them with a course code.
